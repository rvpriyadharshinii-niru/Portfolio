The real problem

A customer conversation doesn't end when the call ends.

After a customer-service conversation, agents often need to document what happened — why the customer called, what was discussed, what actions were taken and what needs to happen next.

GenAI could generate this summary automatically. But there was another problem: different businesses need very different summaries.

Why configuration was needed

The AI could generate a summary. The business still needed to define what “good” looked like.

An insurance company may care about claim details. A telecom company may care about the customer's issue and troubleshooting steps. Another organization may need completely different information.

So the product couldn't simply provide one AI-generated summary format. Enterprise teams needed control over what the AI should capture and how the output should be structured.

Summary Builder

Giving teams a way to configure the summary

Summary Builder gives administrators and business users a structured way to define how an AI-generated conversation summary should work. Instead of changing the underlying AI system, users configure the behaviour through the product.

The prompt experience

Making AI configuration feel like product configuration

Advanced Mode exposes more of the instructions given to the model. Instead of expecting users to manually construct everything from scratch, the interface provides separate areas for different kinds of instructions and allows supported variables or information to be inserted into the prompt.

Testing

Configuration wasn't enough. Users needed confidence that it worked.

Because the output is generated by AI, simply saving a question or template doesn't guarantee that it will behave as expected. Users need a way to test the configuration using realistic conversation data before publishing it.

With AI, the same kind of configuration doesn't always behave like a fixed rule. Testing gives users a way to check the result before putting it into use.

Impact

From manual documentation to configurable AI assistance

The broader product capability was designed to reduce the manual effort involved in documenting customer conversations by generating summaries automatically. The configuration experience allowed enterprise teams to adapt those summaries to their own requirements instead of relying on one fixed output.

Question Manager further allowed the information being captured to become reusable, testable and business-specific.
