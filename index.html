Hi, I'm Priyadharshini

A Product Designer who enjoys turning complex problems into simple, intuitive experiences.

Over the past 4 years, I've worked across AI, enterprise SaaS, ERP, and B2B products — from AI agents and conversational experiences to analytics, administration, and configuration-heavy platforms. My work sits at the intersection of user needs, business goals, and technology, and more recently, AI-native design workflows using tools like Claude Code and GitHub.
