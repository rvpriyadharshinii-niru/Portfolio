Hi, I'm Priyadharshini

A Product Designer who enjoys turning complex problems into simple, intuitive experiences.

Over the past 4 years, I've worked across enterprise SaaS, AI, ERP, and B2B products, designing everything from AI agents and conversational experiences to analytics, administration, and configuration-heavy platforms.

I'm naturally curious about how things work, especially when a product feels complicated. I like breaking down messy workflows, understanding what users actually need, and finding ways to make the experience feel clearer and more effortless.

My work sits at the intersection of user needs, business goals, and technology. I enjoy thinking through information architecture and interaction flows, designing systems that scale, and working closely with product and engineering teams to take an idea from a rough problem to a usable product.

More recently, I've also been exploring AI-native design workflows, using tools like Claude Code and GitHub to prototype ideas faster and bring design closer to implementation.

At the heart of it, I'm a designer who likes asking “Why does this have to be complicated?” — and then figuring out how to make it simpler.
